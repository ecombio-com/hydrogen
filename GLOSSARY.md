# Ecombio Storefront

The customer-facing shop for Ecombio: how products are grouped, browsed, merchandised and bought.

## Collections & browsing

**Collection**:
A merchandised group of products that shoppers can visit at its own URL.
_Avoid_: Category (unless meaning a Hub), product list

**Hub**:
A top-level Collection covering a whole product area: it has its own product range and also leads shoppers into its Sub-collections. A Hub never sits under another Hub, so the hierarchy is only two levels deep.
_Avoid_: Parent category, category page, parent collection

**Sub-collection**:
A Collection listed under a Hub. It may be listed under more than one Hub, but is never a Hub itself.
_Avoid_: Child collection, subcategory

**Canonical Parent**:
The single Hub a Sub-collection officially belongs to, used for its breadcrumb and search-engine hierarchy, even when other Hubs also list it.
_Avoid_: Parent, primary hub

## Merchandising

**Deal**:
A product currently priced below its compare-at price.
_Avoid_: Sale item, offer, promo

**Saving**:
The amount a Deal is cheaper by, in currency (compare-at price minus current price), not as a percentage.
_Avoid_: Discount, markdown

**Picked Deals**:
The Deals a merchandiser hand-selects for a Collection. When present, they replace the Collection's automatic choice of biggest-Saving Deals.
_Avoid_: Featured deals, curated deals
